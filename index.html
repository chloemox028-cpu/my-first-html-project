Pineapple Turnover Cake

A tropical twist on a classic dessert.

About This Recipe

The pineapple turnover cake is a timeless dessert loved for its caramelized pineapple topping, tender crumb, and sweet tropical flavor. It’s perfect for family gatherings, celebrations, or just a treat yourself moment.

Ingredients

Topping:

  • ¼ cup (60 g) unsalted butter, melted
  • ½ cup (100 g) brown sugar
  • 7–8 pineapple slices
  • Maraschino cherries (optional)

Cake Batter:

  • 1 ½ cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 ½ tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ cup (115 g) butter, softened
  • 1 cup (200 g) sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ cup (120 ml) pineapple juice
  • ½ cup (120 ml) milk

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9-inch cake pan.
  2. Pour melted butter and brown sugar in the pan, arrange pineapple slices and cherries.
  3. Mix flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  4. Cream butter and sugar until fluffy, then add eggs and vanilla.
  5. Alternate adding flour mix with pineapple juice and milk.
  6. Spread batter over pineapples and bake 40–45 minutes.
  7. Cool for 10 minutes, invert onto a plate, and serve warm or cooled.

Why You will Love This Cake

  • Moist, buttery crumb with a tropical flavor.
  • Impressive presentation with caramelized pineapple topping.
  • Easy to make with simple ingredients.
  • Perfect dessert for parties, holidays, or any sweet craving.
